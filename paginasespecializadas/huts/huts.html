REFUGEES OF THE PYRENEES

Discover and explore different types of shelters in the Pyrenees with our interactive map.

Main Features

The interactive map allows you to:

  • View locations of various types of shelters such as refuges, cabins, and ruins.
  • Get detailed information such as altitude, capacity in summer and winter, and more.
  • Filter shelters by type to explore only what interests you.

Color System

Each type of shelter is marked with a unique color for quick identification:

  • Open shelter (Blue)
  • Closed shelter (Red)
  • Open shelter occupied by shepherd in summer (Orange)
  • Stone shelter (Yellow)
  • Summer-manned refuge, partly open in winter (Light Green)
  • Year-round manned refuge (Pink)
  • Ruin (Gray)

These colors help you quickly identify the type of shelter on the map.
